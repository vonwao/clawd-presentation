## What Original Does Better

- **More thorough.** 15 slides cover every angle: "general = transferable" gets its own SVG diagram, and the "What to do with this" slide gives the audience explicit take-home questions.
- **Safer opening.** Abstract hook ("brain in a jar") works in any room — no personal risk.
- **"General ≠ omniscient" slide.** The SVG showing one system connecting to 8 domains is a strong visual that Alt A drops.
- **Before/Now slide** is clean and immediate at slide 3 — the core shift is established in the first 2 minutes.

## What Alt A Does Better

- **Emotional hook.** Personal story as cold open is more memorable and creates instant connection.
- **Philosophy lands harder.** By asking "What should never be delegated?" BEFORE explaining how agents work, the audience sits with the human disorientation — not just the mechanics.
- **Tighter.** 13 slides, no filler. Every slide advances the arc.
- **OpenClaw is named.** Makes it concrete and authentic rather than generic "an agent."
- **New visual components.** Agent notification cards, pill cloud for creative uses, trust pillar cards — more visual variety than the original.
- **"Then I started wondering" slide (3)** is the strongest individual slide — the pill cloud of creative uses with the punchline "Not replacing me. Replacing the version of me that was just grinding."

## Recommendation

- **Use Original** for formal or unfamiliar audiences where you want maximum safety and completeness.
- **Use Alt A** for audiences where you can be personal, and where a tighter, more emotional arc will land better.
