# Image Prompts for Clawd Presentation

**Style guide:** Cinematic photography, dark moody tones, blue-black palette with subtle red accents, shallow depth of field, minimal compositions with generous negative space. 16:9.

---

## Slide 1: Title — `00-title.jpg`
```
Cinematic photography. Dark ocean surface at night, a single lobster claw breaking through the water from below, lit by cold moonlight. Minimal, ominous, beautiful. Deep blue-black water, subtle red on the claw. Wide shot with negative space in upper half for title text. 16:9, presentation quality.
```

## Slide 2: Brain in a Jar — `01-brain-in-jar.jpg`
```
Cinematic still life photography. A luminous human brain suspended in a large glass bell jar, sitting on a dark wooden surface. The brain glows with soft blue-white bioluminescence. Dark background, dramatic side lighting. Mood: beautiful but isolated — intelligence without agency. 16:9, presentation quality.
```

## Slide 5: Empty Office — `04-empty-office.jpg`
```
Editorial photography. A modern minimalist office at dusk. One desk, one laptop open with screen glowing, an ergonomic chair slightly pulled back as if someone just left — or was never there. No people. Floor-to-ceiling windows showing a city skyline at blue hour. Slightly eerie, liminal. Cool blue-gray palette. Wide shot. 16:9, presentation quality.
```

## Slide 8: Texture of the Future — `07-texture-future.jpg`
```
Abstract cinematic photography. Morning light filtering through sheer curtains onto a desk with a coffee cup, phone, and laptop. Everything looks normal, but there's a subtle uncanny quality — the screen shows work being done with no one at the keyboard. Warm golden light meets cool blue screen glow. Intimate, quiet, slightly surreal. 16:9, presentation quality.
```

## Slide 9: Uncanny Middle — `08-uncanny-middle.jpg`
```
Cinematic photography. A long empty hallway in a modern office building, fluorescent lights creating pools of light and shadow. One door slightly ajar at the end. Liminal space aesthetic. Cool blue-green tones, slightly desaturated. No people. The feeling of presence without a person. Wide shot, centered perspective. 16:9, presentation quality.
```

## Slide 13: Closing — `12-closing.jpg`
```
Cinematic photography. Two hands reaching toward each other — one human hand and one mechanical/robotic hand — in dark space, lit by a single warm light source from above. Not touching yet. Inspired by Michelangelo's Creation of Adam but modern and restrained. The mechanical hand should be elegant, not threatening. Warm light, dark background. 16:9, presentation quality.
```
