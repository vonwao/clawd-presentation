What people argue about

Capability — What can it do?

Agency — Can it act?

Autonomy — Does it act alone?

Reliability — Can we trust it?

Define the five terms quickly. Land this line: "Most AGI debates mix five separate variables into one word."

Agency changes your Tuesday

Give one concrete workflow example: inbox, calendar, follow-up. Land: "Agency is what changes your Tuesday morning."

The architecture of doing

Run this in four beats: task, before, after, delta. Mention guardrails: source links and human approval. Land: "~2.5 hours to ~35 minutes, with better focus on judgment."

General ≠ omniscient

General means transferable

"General means transferable, not omniscient." One system can move across domains by reasoning over goals and tools.

Small shifts in friction
