What I mean by "hands"

write / edit — docs, code, messages

click / browse — research and navigation

run tools — scripts, terminals, workflows

call APIs — software talking to software

loop — try, observe, adjust, repeat

Keep this concrete. "Hands means tool access plus loops, not just better text."

Agency changes your Tuesday

Give one concrete workflow example: inbox, calendar, follow-up. Land: "Agency is what changes your Tuesday morning."

The architecture of doing

General ≠ omniscient

General means transferable

"General means transferable, not omniscient." One system can move across domains by reasoning over goals and tools.

Why AGI debates feel stuck

Intelligence — can it reason?

Capability — what can it do?

Agency — can it act?

Autonomy — does it act unasked?

Reliability — can we trust results?

Define each in one line. Land: "One word, five different variables."

The uncanny middle

confident wrong actions

permission creep

quiet failures

accountability blur — who did this?

Name the tension: sounds human, has no stake. Calm tone: this is where guardrails matter.

What it reveals about us

What is judgment vs. routine?

What work is worth doing ourselves?

What should never be delegated?

What does responsibility mean now?

Slow down here. Land: "The bottleneck becomes judgment and responsibility."

The real threshold is trust

"Is it smart?" — not enough

"Can I trust it?" — the real question

Scope — what it is allowed to do

Visibility — what it did and why

Reversibility — can we undo mistakes quickly

Make this practical. Trust is scope + visibility + reversibility.

What to do with this

1. Where do I want agency, and where don't I?

2. What do I require before I trust it?

3. What becomes more valuable in a world of delegation?

Keep it universal: life, work, creativity. End on responsibility, not hype.
